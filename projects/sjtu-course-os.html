Brief

SJTU Course OS — you like sitting in on classes that aren’t “yours,” but the official picker only opens at selection time, and half the good ones never show up for your major. So: a shared layer of where / when / what is actually taught—rooms, hours, the quietly auditable—so more people can find the door. Smart search first; map maybe later.
