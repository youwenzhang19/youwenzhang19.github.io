L1 · Review (raw notes)

Simple picture of a galaxy
- self-bound system constitutes of dark matter, stars(star clusters), gas & dust (ISM), spanning across 10³–10⁵ ly.
- Inner Dynamic picture:
  Gas collapses when it reaches Jeans density. Stars form from the cold gas&dust(ISM). Stars end their life and eject abundant heavy chemicals into ISM by means of stellar winds/planetary nebulae/SN. This process is called chemical enrichment.
- Morphology: elliptical galaxy \ spiral galaxy (S) \ spiral galaxy with a bar (Sb) \ irregular galaxy
  - Elliptical galaxy: red&dead
  - Spiral/disk galaxy: inner red outer blue
- Galaxy Merger
  - Large structure Gravitation Interaction is dominated by the spherical dark halo the galaxy embedded in.
  - Collisionless for stars; Simulated by hydrodynamics(mainly consider gas)
  - Major Merger: nearly equal mass
    Scenario A : passer-by ----- tidal effect forms tidal stellar stream
    Scenario B : interpenetrate ----- ring galaxy form?
Milky Way Overview
- Spiral-bar galaxy
- 0.3 × 30 kpc
- SMBH 4.6×10⁶ M☉
- Structure：
  - Disk (blue, young stars)
  - Bulge (red, elder stars \ SMBH)
  - Halo 10ly（dm、elder star、clusters）
  - Bar 3~4kpc (stars&gas) 20～25degree to earth
  - Satellite galaxy：LMC/SMC
  - Stellar stream: tidal disrupted galaxies/clusters CAN BE USED TO INFER THE GALAXY GRAVITY HISTORY
- Sun: R☉ ~ 8kpc; vel ~ 220 km/s; Period ~ 2×10⁸ yr; Position above the disk
