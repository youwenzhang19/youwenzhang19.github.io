Feeling

Now, as a whole, we existed 1 day ahead of ourselves in the future. The future is the simple truth that we are wandering, drifting beings.
The capsule bearing our history has now become a cocoon, carrying all the future of mankind within itself. From it, new life will emerge, one that does not belong to any of us, either as individuals or as a species.
And in that moment, we shall finally become the sky again: by nature, by matter, and by form.
